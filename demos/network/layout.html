A continuing world

Hover a choice to see the involved cars’ translucent futures. Click it to instruct the real cars, at your current driving speed. Their new positions become the starting point for later decisions. Independent agreements can run together; accepted promises remain binding.

Time changes are predicted local time changes, relative to continuing the existing drive and its accepted agreements. A 25-second forecast uses current positions and announced paths. If the crossing lies beyond that window, the estimate uses remaining distance and recent motion. Other cars and later decisions can change the outcome.

The drive uses the existing bicycle physics. Future cars use a faster lane approximation. Cars send requests when their driving rules predict an avoidable delay. You can accept or decline requests addressed to your car. These requests use transparent rules; this is a model-free interaction prototype.
